# Source Hierarchy

## Tier 1: Authoritative Frameworks and Official Sources

- IPCC reports and glossaries
- UNDRR Sendai Framework and terminology
- NIST resilience guidance
- OECD resilience systems analysis
- World Bank climate and disaster risk guidance
- Official government or intergovernmental resilience frameworks

## Tier 2: Scientific and Technical Literature

- Peer-reviewed resilience research
- Social-ecological systems literature
- Disaster-risk studies
- Infrastructure resilience studies
- Climate adaptation research
- Network and cascading-failure research

## Tier 3: Applied and Practice Sources

- Practitioner toolkits
- Community resilience guides
- Case studies
- Indicator frameworks
- Scenario planning methods
- Infrastructure and emergency management reports
