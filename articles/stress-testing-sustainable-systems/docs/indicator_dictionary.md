# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| baseline_capacity | Ordinary operating capacity of the system before adverse stress is applied | 0–1 |
| hazard_intensity | Severity of the adverse stress scenario, such as drought, heat, flood, cyber outage, fiscal shock, or compound pressure | 0–1 |
| exposure | Degree to which people, assets, ecosystems, services, or institutions are exposed to the stress scenario | 0–1 |
| social_vulnerability | Susceptibility to harm among affected communities, users, workers, or dependent populations | 0–1 |
| interdependence_exposure | Degree to which the system depends on other systems that may transmit cascading disruption | 0–1 |
| redundancy | Spare capacity, buffers, alternate routes, backup systems, or substitute services | 0–1 |
| recovery_capacity | Ability to restore service, repair damage, mobilize resources, and recover after disruption | 0–1 |
| governance_capacity | Ability to coordinate, decide, fund, communicate, regulate, and adapt under pressure | 0–1 |
| monitoring_maturity | Strength of monitoring, early warning, data quality, scenario tracking, and real-time learning | 0–1 |
| threshold_level | Estimated performance boundary or stress level beyond which service degradation accelerates | 0–1 |
