# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| hazard_pressure | Pressure from climate hazards, disasters, environmental degradation, or other place-based hazards | 0–1 |
| livelihood_stress | Pressure on income, employment, farming, fishing, food systems, or household survival strategies | 0–1 |
| conflict_insecurity_pressure | Pressure from conflict, insecurity, violence, persecution, or protection failure | 0–1 |
| exposure | People, housing, services, land, infrastructure, and livelihoods located in harm's way | 0–1 |
| social_vulnerability | Susceptibility to harm shaped by poverty, health, disability, exclusion, legal barriers, or limited options | 0–1 |
| adaptive_capacity | Ability to adjust, plan, diversify livelihoods, access services, and make informed decisions under stress | 0–1 |
| mobility_resources | Financial, logistical, informational, transportation, documentation, and household resources for safe movement | 0–1 |
| protection_access | Access to legal protection, rights, safe pathways, documentation, humanitarian support, and non-discrimination | 0–1 |
| migration_network_strength | Strength of family, diaspora, labor, community, remittance, and information networks across places | 0–1 |
| destination_service_capacity | Capacity of receiving areas to provide housing, health, education, water, sanitation, livelihoods, and services | 0–1 |
| host_community_support | Support for host communities, social cohesion, local organizations, public finance, and receiving institutions | 0–1 |
| recovery_capacity | Ability to return, integrate, relocate, rebuild livelihoods, recover documentation, and restore wellbeing | 0–1 |
| arrival_pressure | Pressure on destination systems from arrivals, service demand, housing need, or local absorption limits | 0–1 |
