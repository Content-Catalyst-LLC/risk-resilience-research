# Validation Plan

A full applied study should validate:

1. Production data by crop, season, and production type.
2. Local demand estimates by food category and neighborhood.
3. Household food insecurity and fresh-food access measures.
4. Water use, energy use, and land-use efficiency.
5. Soil safety, contamination status, and remediation needs.
6. Crop nutrient density and post-harvest quality.
7. Distribution pathways to households, schools, clinics, shelters, and food hubs.
8. Organic waste and nutrient-recovery data.
9. Land tenure, zoning, and public ownership conditions.
10. Equity impacts, including affordability and neighborhood control.
11. Emergency distribution capacity during supply disruptions.
12. Community review and public accountability.

This scaffold uses synthetic data only and should be treated as a reproducible demonstration, not an operational food-planning model.
