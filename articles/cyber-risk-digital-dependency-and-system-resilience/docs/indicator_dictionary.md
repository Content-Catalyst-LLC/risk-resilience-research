# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| digital_criticality | Importance of the digital system for essential services, safety, public administration, financial continuity, infrastructure, or care | 0–1 |
| threat_pressure | Pressure from ransomware, extortion, credential theft, phishing, denial of service, exploitation, fraud, or hostile actors | 0–1 |
| technical_vulnerability_exposure | Exposure caused by unpatched systems, misconfiguration, insecure APIs, legacy systems, weak segmentation, or insecure code | 0–1 |
| dependency_concentration | Dependence on a small number of cloud platforms, identity providers, vendors, software systems, managed service providers, or common components | 0–1 |
| identity_access_weakness | Weakness in authentication, authorization, privileged access, least privilege, account lifecycle, or identity monitoring | 0–1 |
| vendor_supply_chain_exposure | Exposure through software vendors, integrations, managed service providers, third-party access, and update mechanisms | 0–1 |
| operational_technology_exposure | Exposure of physical operations, industrial control systems, utilities, transport systems, building systems, sensors, or operational processes | 0–1 |
| data_integrity_risk | Risk that data, records, models, logs, or decision inputs are manipulated, corrupted, incomplete, or untrustworthy | 0–1 |
| recovery_capacity | Ability to restore systems, services, data, operations, and public functions after disruption | 0–1 |
| governance_capacity | Capacity for leadership accountability, policy, risk ownership, vendor oversight, funding, compliance, and decision authority | 0–1 |
| backup_redundancy_capacity | Strength of isolated backups, redundancy, alternate systems, manual fallback, and tested restore paths | 0–1 |
| monitoring_maturity | Ability to observe, detect, correlate, and respond to cyber and operational anomalies | 0–1 |
| logging_maturity | Quality, retention, coverage, integrity, and usability of logs for detection, investigation, and recovery | 0–1 |
| incident_exercise_maturity | Quality of incident response exercises, continuity drills, tabletop exercises, and cross-functional recovery practice | 0–1 |
| user_vulnerability | Vulnerability among users or communities affected by digital service failure, exclusion, data loss, fraud, or access disruption | 0–1 |
| dependency_weight | Strength of digital dependence by one system on another system, vendor, platform, data flow, or shared service | 0–1 |
