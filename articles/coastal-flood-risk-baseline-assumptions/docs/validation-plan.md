# Validation Plan

A full applied study should validate:

1. Local vertical datums and geodetic reference systems.
2. Tide-gauge records and measured coastal sea levels.
3. Satellite altimetry and regional sea-level trends.
4. High-resolution coastal elevation data.
5. Land subsidence, uplift, or sediment compaction.
6. Storm-surge, tide, rainfall, river discharge, and wave models.
7. Protective infrastructure heights and condition.
8. Critical infrastructure inventories.
9. Population and social vulnerability data.
10. Uncertainty ranges and sensitivity tests.
11. Public transparency and community review processes.

This scaffold uses synthetic data only and should be treated as a reproducible demonstration, not an operational flood map.
