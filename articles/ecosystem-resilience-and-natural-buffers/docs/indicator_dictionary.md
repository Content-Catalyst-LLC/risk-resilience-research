# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| ecosystem_condition | Overall health and functionality of the ecosystem | 0–1 |
| ecological_connectivity | Habitat, watershed, corridor, or landscape connectivity | 0–1 |
| functional_biodiversity | Biodiversity and ecological functional redundancy | 0–1 |
| maintenance_capacity | Capacity to steward, manage, monitor, and maintain the buffer | 0–1 |
| restoration_investment | Investment in restoration, recovery, or ecosystem repair | 0–1 |
| hazard_pressure | Climate, flood, heat, erosion, storm, landslide, or water-risk pressure | 0–1 |
| exposure | People, assets, infrastructure, ecosystems, and services in harm's way | 0–1 |
| social_vulnerability | Unequal susceptibility and limited capacity to avoid or recover from harm | 0–1 |
| governance_capacity | Institutional ability to protect, coordinate, regulate, and govern ecosystems | 0–1 |
| degradation_pressure | Development, pollution, fragmentation, overuse, or climate stress degrading the ecosystem | 0–1 |
