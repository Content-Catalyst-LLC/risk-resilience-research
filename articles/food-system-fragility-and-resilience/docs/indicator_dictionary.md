# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| production_stress | Crop, livestock, fishery, or harvest pressure | 0–1 |
| water_stress | Water scarcity, drought, flood, or irrigation stress affecting food systems | 0–1 |
| ecological_degradation | Soil, biodiversity, pollinator, forest, fishery, or ecosystem degradation | 0–1 |
| logistics_fragility | Weakness in transport, storage, ports, cold chains, markets, or distribution | 0–1 |
| input_dependency | Dependence on vulnerable fertilizer, fuel, seed, feed, machinery, or finance inputs | 0–1 |
| price_volatility | Instability in food, input, fuel, or market prices | 0–1 |
| household_vulnerability | Household exposure to hunger, poverty, displacement, labor precarity, or income shock | 0–1 |
| inequality_pressure | Unequal exposure, access, protection, and recovery capacity | 0–1 |
| social_protection_capacity | Ability of public and community systems to protect food access | 0–1 |
| nutritional_adequacy | Dietary quality, safety, and nutrition availability | 0–1 |
| food_system_diversity | Diversity of crops, suppliers, markets, knowledge systems, and food pathways | 0–1 |
| ecological_buffer_condition | Condition of soils, watersheds, biodiversity, pollinators, and living buffers | 0–1 |
| governance_capacity | Institutional ability to monitor, coordinate, protect, regulate, and adapt | 0–1 |
| market_access_reliability | Reliability of physical and economic access to food markets | 0–1 |
| storage_capacity | Capacity for grain reserves, cold chains, warehouses, household storage, and public reserves | 0–1 |
| trade_dependency | Exposure to external suppliers, routes, commodities, or market shocks | 0–1 |
| cross_sector_linkage | Degree of dependency across food, energy, water, health, finance, transport, and labor systems | 0–1 |
