# Source Notes

Suggested source families for production use:

- FAO agrifood systems definitions and food systems frameworks
- IPCC AR6 WGII Chapter 5 on food, fibre, and ecosystem products
- WFP food systems and climate-resilient food systems reports
- World Bank Food Systems 2030 and Food Security Update materials
- Crop yield, drought, flood, rainfall, soil, and water datasets
- Food price, transport, logistics, fertilizer, fuel, and trade data
- Household surveys, nutrition indicators, social protection records, and food assistance data
- Market access, storage, cold chain, and public procurement datasets

This sample package uses synthetic data only.
