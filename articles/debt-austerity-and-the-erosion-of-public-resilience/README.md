## Purpose

This folder provides reproducible scaffolding for modeling debt, austerity, and public resilience as relationships among:

- debt service burden
- revenue capacity
- essential service spending
- public investment
- maintenance capacity
- adaptation and disaster-risk-reduction spending
- social protection capacity
- governance capacity
- local government capacity
- essential service cuts
- public investment cuts
- maintenance deferral
- adaptation deferral
- social protection cuts
- public workforce stress
- social vulnerability
- hazard exposure
- inequality pressure
- prior deferred risk
- debt service pressure
- public resilience capacity
- austerity intensity
- fiscal resilience risk
- deferred risk burden
- public resilience gaps
- scenario analysis
- uncertainty simulation

## Languages Included

- Python: advanced debt-austerity and public-resilience diagnostics
- R: dashboard-ready fiscal resilience, austerity, region, context, and scenario summaries
- SQL: schema scaffold for fiscal systems, debt-service indicators, spending records, austerity records, resilience indicators, and source provenance
- Go: lightweight fiscal-resilience scoring service scaffold
- Rust: command-line public-resilience capacity scoring utility
- C: compact fiscal-resilience risk calculation
- C++: deferred-risk and public-resilience gap example
- Fortran: numerical public-resilience gap example

The examples use synthetic data and are intended for conceptual, educational, and research-scaffolding purposes.
