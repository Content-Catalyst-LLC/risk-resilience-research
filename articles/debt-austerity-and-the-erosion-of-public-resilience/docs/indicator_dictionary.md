# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| debt_service_burden | Pressure from interest and principal payments relative to public resources | 0–1 |
| revenue_capacity | Capacity to raise public revenue through stable, fair, and administratively feasible systems | 0–1 |
| essential_service_spending | Strength of spending on health, education, water, sanitation, care, and basic services | 0–1 |
| public_investment | Strength of public investment in infrastructure, institutions, and long-term capacity | 0–1 |
| maintenance_capacity | Ability to maintain roads, bridges, water systems, schools, hospitals, grids, and public assets | 0–1 |
| adaptation_drr_spending | Spending on climate adaptation, disaster-risk reduction, preparedness, and risk prevention | 0–1 |
| social_protection_capacity | Capacity of cash transfers, food support, unemployment support, pensions, housing support, and care systems | 0–1 |
| governance_capacity | Administrative and institutional capacity to plan, coordinate, fund, procure, and deliver public functions | 0–1 |
| local_government_capacity | Fiscal and administrative capacity of local governments and frontline public institutions | 0–1 |
| essential_service_cuts | Austerity pressure applied to essential services | 0–1 |
| public_investment_cuts | Austerity pressure applied to capital investment and public development capacity | 0–1 |
| maintenance_deferral | Degree to which infrastructure maintenance and public-asset repair are delayed | 0–1 |
| adaptation_deferral | Degree to which climate adaptation and disaster-risk reduction are delayed | 0–1 |
| social_protection_cuts | Austerity pressure applied to household support, care systems, and social safety nets | 0–1 |
| public_workforce_stress | Stress on public workers through hiring freezes, wage compression, burnout, or understaffing | 0–1 |
| social_vulnerability | Susceptibility to harm caused by poverty, exclusion, health burdens, housing insecurity, or limited options | 0–1 |
| hazard_exposure | Exposure to climate, disaster, health, infrastructure, food, water, and economic shocks | 0–1 |
| inequality_pressure | Unequal distribution of austerity burdens, debt adjustment, exposure, protection, and recovery | 0–1 |
| prior_deferred_risk | Existing backlog of underinvestment, maintenance delay, adaptation delay, and public-system fragility | 0–1 |
