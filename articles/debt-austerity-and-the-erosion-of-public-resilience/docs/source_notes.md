# Source Notes

Suggested source families for production use:

- UN Trade and Development debt and development finance reports
- World Bank International Debt Report and debt statistics
- IMF debt sustainability, macro-fiscal resilience, and public investment materials
- UNDP analysis of debt service, gendered impacts, inequality, and development reversals
- National fiscal accounts, public expenditure reviews, budget documents, debt-service records, and public investment plans
- Infrastructure maintenance, health, education, social protection, climate adaptation, disaster-risk reduction, and local government finance records
- Social vulnerability, inequality, hazard exposure, disaster loss, and public service continuity datasets

This sample package uses synthetic data only.
