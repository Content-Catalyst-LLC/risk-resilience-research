# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| concurrent_hazard_intensity | Strength of hazards occurring at the same time | 0–1 |
| sequential_hazard_pressure | Pressure created by hazards occurring in close sequence | 0–1 |
| exposure | People, assets, ecosystems, and institutions in harm's way | 0–1 |
| social_vulnerability | Susceptibility to harm rooted in social and economic conditions | 0–1 |
| infrastructure_fragility | Fragility of built systems under compound stress | 0–1 |
| health_system_strain | Pressure on public-health and healthcare capacity | 0–1 |
| food_water_energy_stress | Stress across connected food, water, and energy systems | 0–1 |
| governance_readiness | Institutional ability to anticipate, coordinate, and respond | 0–1 |
| cross_sector_dependency | Degree of interdependence among sectors | 0–1 |
| recovery_deficit | Lack of resources and capacity for recovery | 0–1 |
| inequality_pressure | Unequal distribution of hazard, vulnerability, and recovery capacity | 0–1 |
| ecological_buffer_condition | Condition of ecosystems that buffer compound hazards | 0–1 |
| social_protection_capacity | Ability to protect people from cascading social harm | 0–1 |
| communication_reliability | Reliability of warning, communication, and coordination systems | 0–1 |
