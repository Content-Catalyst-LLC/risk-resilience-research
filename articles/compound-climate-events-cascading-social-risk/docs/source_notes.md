# Source Notes

Suggested source families for production use:

- IPCC assessment reports and glossaries on climate risk, compound events, and extremes
- UNDRR reports on systemic, cascading, and compound risks
- National Academies reports on compounding and cascading events
- National climate assessments and hazard datasets
- Local emergency management, public health, food, water, energy, and infrastructure data
- Census, social vulnerability, poverty, housing, labor, and public-health records
- Ecological buffer, land-cover, floodplain, wildfire, drought, and heat-island datasets

This sample package uses synthetic data only.
