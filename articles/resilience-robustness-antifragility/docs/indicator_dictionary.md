# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| stress_intensity | Normalized severity of disturbance or stress | 0–1 |
| stress_variability | Degree of volatility, variability, or uncertainty in stress conditions | 0–1 |
| system_criticality | Importance of the system for life, essential service continuity, or cascading risk | 0–1 |
| robustness_capacity | Ability to resist degradation under stress | 0–1 |
| recovery_capacity | Ability to restore function after disruption | 0–1 |
| adaptive_capacity | Ability to adjust, reorganize, or change strategy | 0–1 |
| redundancy_capacity | Spare, backup, substitute, or distributed capacity | 0–1 |
| modularity_capacity | Ability to isolate failure and prevent system-wide spread | 0–1 |
| monitoring_capacity | Ability to observe stress, detect failure, and trigger response | 0–1 |
| learning_capacity | Ability to learn from stress, failures, and variation | 0–1 |
| experimentation_capacity | Ability to test alternatives without catastrophic downside | 0–1 |
| optionality_capacity | Availability of multiple reversible pathways or fallback options | 0–1 |
| failure_containment | Ability to keep failures small, bounded, and non-cascading | 0–1 |
| harm_bounding_capacity | Ability to prevent experimentation or stress from imposing unacceptable harm | 0–1 |
| justice_safeguard_capacity | Ability to protect vulnerable communities from bearing the cost of system learning | 0–1 |
