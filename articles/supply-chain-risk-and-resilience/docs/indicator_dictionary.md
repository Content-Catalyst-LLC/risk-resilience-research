# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| criticality | Importance of the good, input, or component for essential services, safety, health, infrastructure, or public continuity | 0–1 |
| supplier_concentration | Dependence on a small number of suppliers, facilities, regions, or production nodes | 0–1 |
| dependency_intensity | Degree to which downstream systems rely on the item with limited flexibility | 0–1 |
| logistics_exposure | Exposure to transport chokepoints, ports, canals, border crossings, freight volatility, or route disruption | 0–1 |
| cyber_digital_risk | Dependence on digital logistics, platforms, software, data integrity, or cyber-vulnerable coordination systems | 0–1 |
| workforce_vulnerability | Fragility caused by unsafe work, labor shortage, burnout, wage pressure, heat exposure, or precarious labor | 0–1 |
| climate_hazard_exposure | Exposure to drought, flood, heat, wildfire, storm, sea-level, or other climate-related logistics and production risks | 0–1 |
| inventory_buffer | Safety stock, strategic reserves, storage capacity, and available days of supply | 0–1 |
| substitutability | Ease of replacing the item, material, supplier, route, standard, or process | 0–1 |
| supplier_redundancy | Availability of alternative suppliers and production regions | 0–1 |
| modular_production_capacity | Ability to redesign, retool, standardize, or shift production across facilities | 0–1 |
| governance_capacity | Public and private ability to coordinate, regulate, procure, share data, and act under disruption | 0–1 |
| logistics_flexibility | Availability of alternate routes, modes, warehouses, carriers, and delivery strategies | 0–1 |
| recovery_time_pressure | Long lead times, certification delays, restart difficulty, or slow restoration after disruption | 0–1 |
| vulnerable_population_exposure | Degree to which shortages affect people with fewer substitutes, resources, health buffers, or access options | 0–1 |
| essential_service_relevance | Degree to which the item supports health, food, water, energy, infrastructure, emergency services, or public safety | 0–1 |
