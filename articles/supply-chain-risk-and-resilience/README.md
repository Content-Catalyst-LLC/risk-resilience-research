## Purpose

This folder provides reproducible scaffolding for modeling supply chain risk and resilience as relationships among:

- criticality
- supplier concentration
- dependency intensity
- logistics exposure
- cyber-digital risk
- workforce vulnerability
- climate hazard exposure
- inventory buffers
- substitutability
- supplier redundancy
- modular production capacity
- governance capacity
- logistics flexibility
- recovery time pressure
- vulnerable population exposure
- essential service relevance
- disruption pressure
- resilience buffer capacity
- shortage risk
- concentration-adjusted dependency
- service-continuity gaps
- public-priority scoring
- scenario analysis
- uncertainty simulation

## Languages Included

- Python: advanced supply-chain risk and resilience diagnostics
- R: dashboard-ready supply-chain, sector, context, and scenario summaries
- SQL: schema scaffold for items, suppliers, dependencies, logistics routes, inventory records, disruptions, procurement, and source provenance
- Go: lightweight supply-chain resilience scoring service scaffold
- Rust: command-line resilience-buffer capacity scoring utility
- C: compact shortage-risk calculation
- C++: continuity-gap and public-priority scoring example
- Fortran: numerical supply-chain continuity-gap example

The examples use synthetic data and are intended for conceptual, educational, and research-scaffolding purposes.
