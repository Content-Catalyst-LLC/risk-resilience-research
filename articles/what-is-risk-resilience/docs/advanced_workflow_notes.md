# Advanced Risk and Resilience Workflows

This folder extends the article **What Are Risk and Resilience in Sustainable Systems?** with applied analytics that are intentionally more advanced than the article body.

## What the workflows model

The Python and R workflows treat risk as relational rather than event-based. They separate:

- hazard intensity
- exposure
- social vulnerability
- infrastructure vulnerability
- ecological vulnerability
- adaptive capacity
- governance capacity
- ecological buffer capacity
- redundancy
- early-warning capacity
- transformation capacity
- supply-chain dependency
- critical infrastructure dependency
- social inequality

## Why this is more advanced than the article

The article introduces the concepts. The repository operationalizes them.

The workflows produce:

- system-level risk and resilience scores
- justice-adjusted vulnerability diagnostics
- resilience gap analysis
- transformation readiness scores
- intervention scenario comparisons
- domain summaries
- regional summaries
- dashboard-ready long-format exports
- Monte Carlo uncertainty summaries in Python

## Recommended interpretation

These scores are not final truth. They are transparent diagnostic tools. Applied use should replace the sample data with documented indicators, source provenance, uncertainty intervals, stakeholder review, and context-specific weighting.
