# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| conflict_intensity | Intensity of armed conflict, political violence, criminal violence, or localized insecurity | 0–1 |
| governance_capacity | Ability of institutions to coordinate, manage public finance, maintain administration, and respond under stress | 0–1 |
| service_continuity | Continuity of essential services such as health, water, education, civil registration, and local administration | 0–1 |
| institutional_legitimacy | Degree to which institutions are perceived as credible, fair, protective, and accountable | 0–1 |
| administrative_reach | Ability of public systems to reach territories, communities, and service points | 0–1 |
| recovery_capacity | Capacity to restore services, livelihoods, infrastructure, documentation, and public functions after disruption | 0–1 |
| social_vulnerability | Susceptibility to harm due to poverty, exclusion, health burdens, weak services, or limited options | 0–1 |
| displacement_pressure | Pressure from forced displacement, mobility restriction, refugee/IDP needs, or loss of place-based rights | 0–1 |
| livelihood_stress | Stress on income, markets, food systems, farming, labor, trade, and household survival strategies | 0–1 |
| hazard_exposure | Exposure to climate hazards, disasters, disease outbreaks, infrastructure failure, or compound shocks | 0–1 |
| essential_service_demand | Demand pressure on essential public, social, and humanitarian services under stress | 0–1 |
| inequality_pressure | Unequal protection, unequal exposure, exclusion, grievance, or cumulative disadvantage | 0–1 |
| institutional_exclusion | Exclusion from protection, aid, legal status, documentation, participation, or public services | 0–1 |
| public_trust | Trust in public institutions, warnings, services, conflict management, and recovery systems | 0–1 |
| repeated_disruption_pressure | Pressure from repeated shocks, chronic instability, recurring displacement, or slow recovery | 0–1 |
