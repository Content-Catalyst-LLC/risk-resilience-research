# Source Notes

Suggested source families for production use:

- OECD States of Fragility and multidimensional fragility materials
- World Bank Fragility, Conflict, and Violence strategy and FCV data
- UNDP governance, centers of government, local governance, and social-contract materials
- UNDRR disaster-risk reduction and early-warning materials for fragile and conflict-affected contexts
- Conflict-event datasets, displacement data, service-continuity records, local governance assessments, and humanitarian access data
- Food security, water security, public health, climate hazard, and infrastructure disruption records
- Public finance, civil service, administrative reach, social protection, trust, and recovery-program indicators

This sample package uses synthetic data only.
