## Purpose

This folder provides reproducible scaffolding for modeling conflict, fragility, and resilience under stress as relationships among:

- conflict intensity
- governance capacity
- service continuity
- institutional legitimacy
- administrative reach
- recovery capacity
- social vulnerability
- displacement pressure
- livelihood stress
- hazard exposure
- essential service demand
- inequality pressure
- institutional exclusion
- public trust
- fragility pressure
- governance resilience
- conflict-amplified systemic risk
- service breakdown gaps
- legitimacy erosion
- resilience-under-stress gaps
- scenario analysis
- uncertainty simulation

## Languages Included

- Python: advanced conflict-fragility and governance-resilience diagnostics
- R: dashboard-ready fragility, governance, service-continuity, regional, context, and scenario summaries
- SQL: schema scaffold for systems, indicators, conflict events, service records, recovery records, and source provenance
- Go: lightweight conflict-fragility scoring service scaffold
- Rust: command-line fragility-pressure scoring utility
- C: compact conflict-amplified systemic-risk calculation
- C++: legitimacy erosion and resilience-gap example
- Fortran: numerical resilience-under-stress gap example

The examples use synthetic data and are intended for conceptual, educational, and research-scaffolding purposes.
