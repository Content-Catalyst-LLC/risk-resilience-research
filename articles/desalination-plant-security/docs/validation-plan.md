# Validation Plan

A full applied study should validate:

1. Actual desalination production capacity and seasonal variation.
2. Priority demand under normal, drought, heatwave, and emergency conditions.
3. Treated-water storage capacity and usable storage constraints.
4. Alternative sources, including groundwater, reservoirs, reuse, interconnections, and emergency transfers.
5. Power-system reliability and backup-power duration.
6. Repair timelines for membranes, pumps, intake systems, electrical systems, and controls.
7. Spare-parts inventories and supply-chain constraints.
8. Cybersecurity and operational-technology governance through qualified defensive review.
9. Environmental monitoring for intake quality, algal blooms, turbidity, salinity, spills, and brine impacts.
10. Vulnerable populations and priority users such as hospitals, shelters, care facilities, and sanitation systems.
11. Public accountability, legal authority, and emergency communication protocols.

This scaffold uses synthetic data only and should be treated as a reproducible demonstration, not an operational security tool.
