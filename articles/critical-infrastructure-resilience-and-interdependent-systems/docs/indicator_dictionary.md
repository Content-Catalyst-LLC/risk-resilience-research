# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| criticality | Importance of the infrastructure system for safety, health, continuity, economic activity, governance, or emergency response | 0–1 |
| hazard_exposure | Exposure to climate hazards, natural hazards, physical threats, operational stress, or compound events | 0–1 |
| asset_fragility | Vulnerability of physical assets due to age, condition, design limits, location, or maintenance backlog | 0–1 |
| cyber_physical_risk | Risk that digital systems, industrial control systems, communications, cloud services, or data integrity affect physical operations | 0–1 |
| redundancy | Availability of alternate routes, backup systems, substitute facilities, spare capacity, or distributed architecture | 0–1 |
| maintenance_capacity | Capacity to inspect, repair, fund, and maintain infrastructure over its lifecycle | 0–1 |
| governance_capacity | Capacity to regulate, coordinate, fund, oversee, plan, enforce standards, and assign accountability | 0–1 |
| recovery_capacity | Capacity to restore service quickly after disruption through crews, parts, plans, mutual aid, and logistics | 0–1 |
| backup_capacity | Backup power, communications, water storage, data backups, manual fallback, and substitute operations | 0–1 |
| workforce_readiness | Staffing, training, safety, access, surge capacity, and institutional knowledge needed to operate and restore systems | 0–1 |
| service_demand_under_stress | Demand for service during disruption, emergency, heat, flood, outage, evacuation, or public crisis | 0–1 |
| social_vulnerability | Vulnerability among affected users due to poverty, health, disability, isolation, housing, age, or limited backup options | 0–1 |
| dependency_weight | Strength of dependence by one infrastructure system on another system | 0–1 |
