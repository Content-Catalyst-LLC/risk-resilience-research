# Source Notes

Suggested source families for production use:

- WHO health systems resilience and essential public health functions materials
- WHO 2024 implementation guidance on building health system resilience to public health challenges
- OECD health system resilience reports and indicators
- World Bank resilient health systems and health system transformation materials
- UNDRR public health system resilience scorecard materials
- Surveillance, laboratory, vaccination, emergency response, service-continuity, workforce, and supply-chain records
- Public health equity, social vulnerability, climate-health, environmental health, and chronic disease datasets
- Community trust, risk communication, access, and service utilization records

This sample package uses synthetic data only.
