# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| health_hazard_pressure | Pressure from infectious disease, heat, smoke, contaminated water, chronic burden, displacement, or other health threats | 0–1 |
| exposure | People, services, workplaces, schools, facilities, and communities exposed to the health threat | 0–1 |
| social_health_vulnerability | Health and social susceptibility shaped by poverty, housing, disability, chronic disease, labor exposure, or exclusion | 0–1 |
| surveillance_capacity | Ability to monitor, detect, test, report, and interpret emerging health threats | 0–1 |
| prevention_capacity | Ability to prevent avoidable harm through vaccination, sanitation, health promotion, environmental health, and preparedness | 0–1 |
| essential_service_continuity | Ability to maintain routine and essential health services during disruption | 0–1 |
| workforce_capacity | Staffing, training, safety, surge capacity, retention, and worker support | 0–1 |
| supply_chain_reliability | Reliability of medicines, vaccines, diagnostics, PPE, oxygen, cold chains, logistics, and procurement | 0–1 |
| public_trust | Trust in public health institutions, guidance, services, and emergency communication | 0–1 |
| communication_capacity | Ability to communicate clearly, accessibly, multilingualy, and through trusted channels | 0–1 |
| recovery_capacity | Ability to restore services, address deferred care, learn from crisis, and rebuild capacity | 0–1 |
| inequality_pressure | Unequal exposure, access, protection, recovery, and cumulative health disadvantage | 0–1 |
| essential_service_demand | Demand pressure on care, prevention, emergency response, and routine services under stress | 0–1 |
| repeated_health_disruption_pressure | Pressure from repeated health shocks, slow recovery, chronic underinvestment, or recurring service disruption | 0–1 |
