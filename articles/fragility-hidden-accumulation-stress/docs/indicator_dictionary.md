# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| visible_performance | Current visible output or service continuity | 0–1 |
| stress_accumulation | Degree of accumulated hidden pressure | 0–1 |
| buffer_erosion | Loss of slack, reserves, ecological buffers, or spare capacity | 0–1 |
| deferred_maintenance | Maintenance backlog, delayed repair, or underinvestment | 0–1 |
| institutional_drift | Gradual movement away from safer standards or practices | 0–1 |
| signal_normalization | Degree to which warning signs are treated as ordinary | 0–1 |
| standard_erosion | Decline in goals, expectations, or acceptable performance standards | 0–1 |
| threshold_proximity | Nearness to a critical transition or nonlinear regime shift | 0–1 |
| adaptation_debt | Hidden fragility accumulated through workarounds and coping | 0–1 |
| ecological_support_erosion | Decline in ecosystem buffering and regenerative support | 0–1 |
| social_strain | Burden absorbed by households, workers, communities, or public systems | 0–1 |
| trust_erosion | Loss of public, institutional, or operational trust | 0–1 |
| monitoring_capacity | Ability to see weak signals, margins, and slow variables | 0–1 |
| response_capacity | Ability to respond before fragility becomes crisis | 0–1 |
| justice_pressure | Degree to which stress burdens vulnerable groups unevenly | 0–1 |
| system_criticality | Importance of the system for essential service continuity | 0–1 |
