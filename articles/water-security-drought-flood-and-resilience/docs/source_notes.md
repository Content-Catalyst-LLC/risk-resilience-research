# Source Notes

Suggested source families for production use:

- UN-Water water scarcity, water security, water and climate change, and water and peace materials
- IPCC AR6 WGII Chapter 4 on water
- World Bank water security and fragility reports
- FAO agricultural water scarcity and water withdrawal material
- National hydrological, groundwater, reservoir, floodplain, and water-quality datasets
- Water utility service, outage, treatment, infrastructure, and maintenance records
- Social vulnerability, sanitation access, food security, public health, and recovery datasets

This sample package uses synthetic data only.
