# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| drought_pressure | Severity of scarcity, drought, recharge decline, or dry-period stress | 0–1 |
| flood_exposure | Exposure to river, urban, coastal, stormwater, or flash flooding | 0–1 |
| water_demand_pressure | Demand pressure from households, agriculture, industry, energy, and ecosystems | 0–1 |
| water_availability | Reliable available water relative to needs | 0–1 |
| infrastructure_reliability | Condition and reliability of pipes, pumps, storage, drainage, treatment, and flood defenses | 0–1 |
| water_quality | Safety and usability of available water | 0–1 |
| ecosystem_buffer_condition | Condition of wetlands, rivers, forests, soils, aquifers, floodplains, and watersheds | 0–1 |
| governance_capacity | Institutional ability to allocate, communicate, coordinate, regulate, and adapt | 0–1 |
| social_protection_capacity | Ability to protect households and communities during water stress or flood | 0–1 |
| livelihood_water_dependency | Degree to which livelihoods depend on stable water access | 0–1 |
| critical_service_dependence | Degree to which hospitals, schools, food systems, energy, sanitation, and transport depend on water continuity | 0–1 |
| inequality_pressure | Unequal access, exposure, protection, and recovery capacity | 0–1 |
| recovery_capacity | Ability to recover after drought, flood, contamination, or service disruption | 0–1 |
| maintenance_deficit | Deferred repair and maintenance burden in water systems | 0–1 |
| pollution_pressure | Pollution or contamination pressure on usable water | 0–1 |
