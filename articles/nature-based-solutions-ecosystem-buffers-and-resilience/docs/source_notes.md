# Source Notes

Suggested source families for production use:

- IUCN nature-based solutions definition and Global Standard
- IPCC AR6 WGII ecosystem, adaptation, climate risk, and vulnerability chapters
- UNEP ecosystem-based adaptation and nature-based solutions materials
- World Bank and GFDRR nature-based solutions and disaster-risk resilience materials
- Land-cover, NDVI, hydrology, coastal, floodplain, forest, wetland, reef, and urban canopy datasets
- Biodiversity, habitat connectivity, restoration, protected-area, and ecological condition datasets
- Community participation, public finance, maintenance, rights, displacement, and governance records
- Census, social vulnerability, public health, infrastructure exposure, and disaster loss datasets

This sample package uses synthetic data only.
