**Community Resilience, Trust, and Local Capacity**

Article URL:

https://github.com/Content-Catalyst-LLC/risk-resilience-research/tree/main/articles/community-resilience-trust-and-local-capacity

## Purpose

This folder provides reproducible scaffolding for modeling community resilience, trust, and local capacity as relationships among:

- hazard pressure
- exposure
- social vulnerability
- trust level
- local organizational capacity
- mutual aid strength
- communication access
- local knowledge integration
- institutional support
- participation quality
- recovery capacity
- exclusion pressure
- institutional follow-through
- broken-promise pressure
- community hazard pressure
- local resilience capacity
- trust-adjusted response capacity
- participation legitimacy
- community resilience gaps
- updated trust projections
- scenario analysis
- uncertainty simulation

## Languages Included

- Python: advanced community-resilience and trust diagnostics
- R: dashboard-ready local-capacity, trust, participation, regional, context, and scenario summaries
- SQL: schema scaffold for communities, local organizations, trust indicators, participation processes, hazards, recovery records, and source provenance
- Go: lightweight community-resilience scoring service scaffold
- Rust: command-line local-resilience capacity scoring utility
- C: compact trust-adjusted response-capacity calculation
- C++: participation-legitimacy and resilience-gap example
- Fortran: numerical community-resilience gap example

The examples use synthetic data and are intended for conceptual, educational, and research-scaffolding purposes.
