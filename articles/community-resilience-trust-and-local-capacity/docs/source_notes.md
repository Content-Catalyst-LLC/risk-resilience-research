# Source Notes

Suggested source families for production use:

- UNDRR local resilience materials and Making Cities Resilient / local DRR resources
- UNDRR guidance on traditional and Indigenous knowledges for disaster risk reduction
- UNESCO materials on local and Indigenous knowledge for community resilience
- IPCC AR6 materials on inclusive planning informed by Indigenous knowledge, local knowledge, cultural values, and scientific knowledge
- WHO community readiness and community protection/resilience materials
- Community surveys, participatory mapping, local organization inventories, mutual aid network records, and trusted messenger data
- Public health, climate risk, disaster loss, recovery program, social vulnerability, and service-access datasets

This sample package uses synthetic data only.
