# Indicator Dictionary

| Indicator | Meaning | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| hazard_pressure | Intensity, likelihood, or severity of hazards affecting the community | 0–1 |
| exposure | People, housing, services, organizations, assets, and lifelines located in harm's way | 0–1 |
| social_vulnerability | Susceptibility to harm shaped by poverty, housing, health, disability, exclusion, or limited options | 0–1 |
| trust_level | Trust in public institutions, local organizations, warnings, services, and communication channels | 0–1 |
| local_organizational_capacity | Capacity of local organizations, civic groups, clinics, schools, faith groups, local governments, and community institutions | 0–1 |
| mutual_aid_strength | Strength of reciprocal support, informal care networks, volunteer coordination, and community response | 0–1 |
| communication_access | Access to trusted, multilingual, disability-accessible, practical, and timely communication | 0–1 |
| local_knowledge_integration | Degree to which local, Indigenous, place-based, and experiential knowledge are integrated into decisions | 0–1 |
| institutional_support | Material, financial, technical, and legal support from public institutions and partners | 0–1 |
| participation_quality | Meaningfulness of participation, shared authority, accessibility, compensation, and decision influence | 0–1 |
| recovery_capacity | Ability to recover services, livelihoods, housing, relationships, and community functions after disruption | 0–1 |
| exclusion_pressure | Degree to which voices, groups, or needs are excluded from planning, aid, services, or authority | 0–1 |
| institutional_follow_through | Degree to which institutions keep commitments and deliver promised support | 0–1 |
| broken_promise_pressure | Pressure from failed commitments, extractive consultation, abandoned plans, or repeated institutional neglect | 0–1 |
